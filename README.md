# 🍦 MixITup Ice Cream – Customer Segmentation & Business Plan

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on customer data analysis and business strategy development for **MixITup Ice Cream**, a fictional business specializing in ice cream, boba drinks, and desserts.

The project aims to understand customer characteristics, purchasing preferences, and spending behavior through data analysis. The insights are then used to develop customer segmentation and data-driven marketing strategies to support business growth and improve customer engagement.

This project combines two main components:
1. **Data Analytics:** Customer data preprocessing, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and customer segmentation using clustering algorithms.
2. **Business Planning:** Developing marketing strategies, promotional programs, and business expansion recommendations based on customer insights.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Identify customer characteristics and purchasing behavior.
- Explore customer demographics, spending patterns, and product preferences.
- Perform customer segmentation using clustering techniques.
- Compare different clustering approaches.
- Develop targeted marketing and promotional strategies.
- Provide business recommendations to support customer retention and business expansion.

## 📊 Dataset Description

The dataset contains customer information with the following variables:

| Category | Variables |
|---|---|
| Demographics | Gender, Age, Marital Status, City |
| Financial | Monthly Income, Spending Score |
| Customer Preferences | Favorite Day, Favorite Ice Cream Flavor |
| Purchasing Behavior | Promotion Usage |

## 🔍 Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning & Preprocessing
Performed data quality checks and preprocessing, including:
- Removing irrelevant ID columns.
- Identifying and handling duplicate records.
- Correcting inconsistent and invalid values.
- Handling missing values.
- Converting variables into appropriate data types.
- Detecting and handling outliers using the Interquartile Range (IQR) method.

### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Conducted exploratory analysis to understand customer characteristics and behavior through:
- Descriptive statistical analysis.
- Customer age and income distributions.
- Spending score analysis.
- Customer demographics by gender, marital status, and city.
- Promotional preferences and shopping patterns.
- Favorite ice cream flavors and preferred shopping days.

### 3. Customer Segmentation

Applied three clustering algorithms to identify customer groups:

- **K-Means++**
- **K-Medoids**
- **K-Prototypes**

The Elbow Method was used to explore the optimal number of clusters. Four customer clusters were selected for further analysis.

The clustering results were compared through visualizations of customer characteristics and cluster distributions. K-Means++ was selected for the subsequent customer profiling based on the visual comparison conducted in the project.

### 4. Customer Profiling

Four customer segments were identified:

| Segment | Customer Characteristics |
|---|---|
| Type 1 | Older customers (40–60 years), higher income, and relatively conservative spending behavior. |
| Type 2 | Younger customers (20–40 years), middle-income levels, higher spending scores, and frequent promotion usage. |
| Type 3 | The most dominant customer segment, with diverse ages, income levels, and relatively balanced spending behavior. |
| Type 4 | Younger customers (20–40 years), lower income, and lower spending scores. |

These profiles provide a basis for developing marketing strategies tailored to different customer groups.

## 💡 Business Insights & Recommendations

Based on the customer analysis, several marketing and business development strategies were proposed.

### 1. Targeted Marketing & Promotions
- Introduce gender-based promotional packages.
- Provide discounts based on minimum purchase amounts.
- Offer special promotions on days with relatively lower customer visits.
- Implement loyalty programs to encourage repeat purchases.

### 2. Product Cross-Selling
- Combine popular ice cream flavors with less-preferred flavors.
- Develop promotional bundles based on customer preferences.
- Introduce special product combinations for different customer segments and branch locations.

### 3. Promotional Campaigns
- Offer special discounts on Wednesday and Thursday.
- Introduce Friday Happy Hour promotions.
- Provide bonus loyalty points on selected promotional days.
- Offer free mini products for purchases above a specified amount.

### 4. Customer Loyalty Programs
Proposed a point-based reward system:
- Every Rp10,000 spent earns 1 point.
- 50 points: 5% discount.
- 100 points: 10% discount and a free mini product.
- 200 points: 20% discount and a free drink.
- 350 points: Free package for two people.

### 5. Business Expansion Strategy
Proposed business development initiatives, including:
- Expanding marketing activities through social media.
- Introducing new and trending ice cream flavors.
- Improving store facilities and customer experience.
- Exploring potential new branch locations in Jabodetabek, Yogyakarta, and Bandung.

## 📈 Project Outcomes

- Performed customer data preprocessing and exploratory analysis.
- Applied and compared three clustering approaches.
- Identified four customer segments based on demographic and spending characteristics.
- Developed customer profiles to support targeted marketing.
- Proposed promotional, cross-selling, and customer loyalty strategies.
- Developed business expansion recommendations based on the analytical findings.
